Compress Photo Support

Compress Photo is a private, local photo-compression app for iPhone.

Common questions

  • Photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Use High Quality for clear smaller photos.
  • Use Tiny for upload limits or smaller file targets.
  • Use Custom to choose JPG, PNG, or HEIC plus dimensions and quality.
  • Use Save to Photos to add compressed files back to your photo library.
